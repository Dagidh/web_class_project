Standard Workshop Price List

All pieces are made to order. Prices vary based on timber selection and custom dimensions.

Choosing the right wood is the most important step in building furniture that fits both your home and your budget. we offer a curated selection of native and regional timbers known for their distinct grain patterns, natural colors, and structural strength.

The starting prices listed in the table below reflect standard residential dimensions and our signature three-coat matte varnish finish. If your space requires extra length, built-in storage drawers, custom wood staining, or intricate hand-carved details, our carpenters will adjust the build plan and provide a clear, itemized quote before cutting a single board. Browse our standard models below to compare estimated build times and timber options, then reach out through our quote page when you are ready to start your project.
